Sources, coverage & privacy

Read the dates correctly

Ubuntu and Debian support dates come from Debian’s distro-info-data. Ubuntu ESM and legacy dates may require registration or an eligible support service. Debian LTS and ELTS have different package coverage and eligibility. AlmaLinux and Rocky Linux active/security dates come from official release documents; Alpine dates come from its release table and apply to main, with shorter community support. Fedora dates are not captured here. Rolling releases have no fixed end date in this tool; keep them updated. Debian unstable is a development branch, not a stable rolling distribution. Individually reviewed policies retain their review date, including the openSUSE Leap 15.6 end date.

Repology is an index of package repositories, not a dependency solver. Missing records mean missing captured evidence, not proof that a package cannot be installed. Repository branches, modules and alternative builds may coexist. Architecture is not available in this source. Always check the distribution’s own package and security documentation before migration.

What leaves your browser

The published capture loads from this site. A new project lookup or refresh sends the project identifier to CoolCode’s cached Repology gateway. Your selected releases, migration notes and imported plans stay in this browser. Nothing is saved locally until you select “Save in this browser”. Imported and saved plans preserve their original evidence; reload to return to the current published capture.
